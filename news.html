Key Takeaway: Industrial Scale vs. Humanoid Reality Check

While venture investment in bipedal humanoid robots (Tesla Optimus, Figure, Unitree) reached $3.8B, traditional articulated industrial arms represent the overwhelming 98%+ of operational shop floor value ($18.5B market). China's rapid import substitution (55% domestic supplier share) and Brazil's automotive boom (+212% EV assembly robotics) highlight where real manufacturing productivity gains are being realized today.
